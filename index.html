Why Eco-Friendy Route Planning?

Traffic congestion causes longer travel times, increased fuel consumption and higher vehicle emissions.

EcoRoute helps users compare alternative routes by considering travel time, distance, traffic, fuel consumption and estimated CO₂ emissions.

Who Can Use EcoRoute?

  • Daily commuters
  • Students
  • Private vehicle users
  • Travellers looking for more sustainable routes

How EcoRoute Works

  1. Enter your journey details.
  2. Compare available route options.
  3. View travel time, fuel and emission estimates.
  4. Get the recommended eco-route.

What Makes EcoRoute Different?

Traditional route planning often focuses mainly on distance and travel time. EcoRoute also considers traffic conditions, fuel consumption and estimated CO₂ emissions.

By combining these factors into an Eco-Score, the system helps users make more environmentally conscious travel decisions without ignoring practical travel efficiency.
