References

The following sources were used to support the environmental background and emission calculations used in the EcoRoute prototype.

  1. World Health Organization (WHO). Transport: Sectoral Solutions for Air Pollution and Health. 2025.
    WHO Transport and Health
  2. United Nations. Sustainable Development Goal 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities.
    UN Sustainable Development Goal 11
  3. International Organization for Standardization (ISO). ISO 14001:2026 – Environmental Management Systems. 2026.
    ISO 14001
  4. International Organization for Standardization (ISO). ISO 14040:2006 – Environmental Management – Life Cycle Assessment – Principles and Framework.
    ISO 14040
  5. U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA). Greenhouse Gas Emissions from a Typical Passenger Vehicle.
    EPA Vehicle Emissions

Note: Route information and vehicle fuel-efficiency values used in the current prototype are sample assumptions. The system does not currently use live GPS, traffic or navigation data.
